Privacy Policy

Weatherland · last updated 1 September 2026

Weatherland is a desktop wallpaper application. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing you do in the app is reported to its developer.

What stays on your computer

Everything the app remembers is stored locally, in the application's own folder, and never leaves the device:

  • the place you chose to display (its name and coordinates) and your scene, palette, camera and widget settings;
  • map data for the cities you display, cached so the app works offline;
  • elevation tiles, cached for the same reason;
  • a plain-text log of what the app did, kept so that problems can be diagnosed on your own machine.

Uninstalling Weatherland removes all of it.

What leaves your computer

To draw real weather over a real place, the app requests public data from the services below. Each request carries the coordinates of the place you chose and, unavoidably, your IP address — as any web request does. No identifier of you or of your device is attached.

ServiceWhat is requestedWhen
open-meteo.comweather forecastabout once an hour
api.met.noweather forecastabout once an hour
geocoding-api.open-meteo.comcity search resultswhen you search for a city
overpass-api.de and its mirrorsOpenStreetMap geometry of a cityonly when you add a city that is not already included
s3.amazonaws.com/elevation-tiles-prodpublic elevation tileswhen a place is first displayed
ipapi.coan approximate city for your IP addressonce, on first run, before you have chosen a place

The request to ipapi.co exists only so that the first scene is somewhere plausible rather than blank. Its answer is stored locally, it is never repeated once you have chosen a place, and choosing a city yourself makes it moot.

Requests to api.met.no carry the developer's own name and contact address in the User-Agent header. Their terms of service require it. That is the developer's data, not yours.

What is never done

Weatherland does not collect personal information, does not build a profile, does not track usage, and does not sell or share data with anyone. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
