Weatherland

A live desktop wallpaper for Windows: your city in 3D, under the weather it actually has.

Buildings are extruded from OpenStreetMap outlines and lit by the real position of the sun. Clouds, rain and wind come from live forecasts. There is also a globe mode — the whole Earth, with real night lights, snow cover and moving weather.

Weatherland is distributed through the Microsoft Store.

Data sources

Map geometry from OpenStreetMap contributors. Weather from Open-Meteo and MET Norway. Night lights, snow cover and imagery from NASA Visible Earth and NASA Earth Observatory (public domain). Star positions from the HYG database.
